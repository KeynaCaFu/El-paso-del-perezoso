Field notes

A wooden retreat, surrounded by tropical forest

Here you can slow down: no TV or Wi-Fi competing for your attention, just the forest, a small pond, and a mini farm as your only agenda. Ideal as a stop on the way to Tortuguero, or as a destination in itself for a weekend with family, friends, or as a couple.
