About the Space

A wooden cabin surrounded by nature and tranquility—perfect for a stop on the way to or from Tortuguero National Park. Relax and enjoy the simple beauty of our home.

The Lodging

Set amid lush tropical flora and fauna, El Paso del Perezoso offers a serene refuge immersed in nature. Tall trees sway with the breeze forming a soothing canopy, while colorful flowers attract hummingbirds and butterflies. If you are lucky, you may spot a sloth resting quietly in the branches.

Guest Access

Our wooden cabin has two floors. The first floor hosts guests: a bedroom with a double bed and mosquito net, a wall fan, and a bathroom with shower. There is also a long terrace and a dining area.

The kitchen is equipped with utensils, drinkable water, microwave, refrigerator, small electric stove, pans, and a coffee maker. Outside, a traditional wood-fired stove is available under host supervision.

The second floor is under construction to enable more space in the future; currently you can access it to rest on the balcony overlooking the property. Guests can explore the garden with abundant flora and fauna, a small pond with ducks and geese, fruit trees, and the chance to see iguanas and sloths around.
